ABOUT THE PROJECT

Creating a
Narrative Website

See for yourself

My website is a personal exploration of Barcelona, designed to capture the city’s spirit through storytelling and imagery. From the moment users arrive, I invite them to join me in discovering its golden beaches, Gothic lanes, and rich Catalan culture. I built a clean, intuitive layout with sections like Explore, Culture, Food & Drink, and Stay to guide the journey. Each page reflects my fascination with Barcelona’s mix of history and modern life. Through words and visuals, I aim to share not just information, but my own sense of wonder, inviting others to experience the city as I did.
