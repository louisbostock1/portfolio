Case Study · September 2023

Barcelona Travel Guide — Editorial Web

I created a narrative travel website that captures Barcelona through storytelling, imagery, and a simple guided structure.

Role
UX & Dev
Context
University Project
Focus
Narrative Website

This project was a personal exploration of Barcelona, designed to capture the city’s spirit through editorial storytelling and imagery.


I built a clean, intuitive layout with sections such as Explore, Culture, Food & Drink, and Stay, guiding users through the city’s beaches, Gothic streets, and Catalan identity.

See the live project ↗

01 — Visual Identity

A city-led palette with cultural energy

The visual identity was influenced by FC Barcelona’s club colours, using deep red, blue, yellow, and black to create a bold editorial feel.

02 — The Challenge

Turning a place into a structured digital journey

  • Content — Balancing practical information with personal storytelling
  • Structure — Organising varied travel themes into a clear route
  • Visuals — Creating atmosphere without overwhelming the text
  • Navigation — Helping users move through the city narrative easily

03 — The Outcome

A narrative website shaped around discovery

  • UX — Simple sections built around common travel interests
  • Design — Strong imagery and confident city-inspired colours
  • Build — A responsive HTML and CSS site
  • Tone — Personal writing that invites users into the experience
