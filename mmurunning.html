Social Media Manager

I first joined MMU Running in January 2024 as their Social Media Manager, a role in which I not only managed the social media accounts such as Instagram & TikTok, but also curated professional content to be used across multiple platforms.

I ensured brand consistency, designed the logo which is still in use today & even fascilitated the creation of merch for our committee and run club members.

Following an election with our 500+ members, I was voted in as Chairman in January 2025 and have retained that role since, across two academic years.
