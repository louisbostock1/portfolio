Case Study · 2024–Present

Knowles Green — Wine Shop

I designed and developed a boutique e-commerce experience for a wine and cheese delicatessen, combining refined visual identity with a fast, responsive build.

Role
UX & Dev
Client
Knowles Green
Stack
HTML · CSS · JS

I designed and developed the website for Knowles Green — a boutique wine and cheese delicatessen in Cheshire. The goal was an elegant, user-friendly platform that reflects the brand's focus on quality and hospitality.


Working closely with the owners, I combined storytelling with usability — using refined design, strong typography, and clear navigation to translate the in-store experience into a digital product.

Shopify · Ongoing

Built and maintained on Shopify

Alongside the design and front-end work, I built the full Knowles Green store on Shopify — setting up the product catalogue, collections, and checkout flow. I continue to maintain the store today, handling updates, seasonal changes, and ongoing improvements as the business grows.

01 — Visual Identity

Earthy sophistication, refined in every detail

The identity uses deep green tones, warm orange accents, and a soft cream base — reflecting tradition, quality, and warmth.

02 — The Problem

The old site was losing customers

  • Visual — Inconsistent branding across pages
  • Layout — Content breaking across screen sizes
  • UX — Navigation unclear and difficult to use
  • Mobile — No responsive experience
  • Performance — Slow load times from unoptimised media

03 — The Solution

A complete rebuild from scratch

  • Design — Consistent visual system across all pages
  • Type — Responsive typography for all devices
  • UX — Clear navigation and improved structure
  • Build — Fully responsive layout
  • Performance — Optimised assets and cleaner codebase
